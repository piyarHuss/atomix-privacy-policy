Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2024

Welcome to Atomix Miner ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and cloud mining network.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google, Email, or Phone OTP, we collect basic profile details (such as your name, email address, phone number, and unique User ID) to manage your in-app account and token balance.
  • App Usage & Mining Data: Mining sessions, rewards, level upgrades, and transaction logs are stored securely using Google Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Device & Ad Identifiers: We may collect non-personally identifiable device information and Advertising IDs to display reward video ads.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate, maintain, and secure the cloud mining simulation environment.
  • To prevent duplicate/fraudulent multi-accounts and automated bot activities.
  • To synchronize your virtual balances and transaction history across devices.
  • To display sponsored ads and reward you with in-app tokens.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app utilizes trusted third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: Used for authentication and real-time database storage.
  • Ad Networks: Used to serve rewarded video ads to unlock in-app perks.

4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us with your personal information. We implement industry-standard security measures and cloud-level encryption to safeguard your data against unauthorized access.

5. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Simply contact us at our support email with your registered UID/Email, and your data will be permanently removed within 30 days.
