5. Wallet, Token Transfers and Withdrawals

Atomix Miner may provide in-app wallet, token balance, token transfer, transaction history, and withdrawal features.

  • Minimum Token Transfer: The minimum amount required for a token transfer is 10,000 tokens.
  • Minimum Withdrawal: A minimum balance of 10,000 tokens may be required before a withdrawal request can be submitted.
  • Withdrawal Processing: Withdrawal requests generally require approximately 3–5 business days for review and processing. Processing may take longer in cases involving verification, security checks, system maintenance, high request volume, technical issues, or other circumstances.
  • Transaction Records: We may store transaction amounts, token type, transaction status, dates, withdrawal records, and other information necessary to operate and review wallet activity.
  • Security Review: Transfers and withdrawals may be reviewed for fraud, abuse, duplicate accounts, automated activity, manipulation, or violations of the application's rules.
Important: Token balances, rewards, and other digital assets displayed in Atomix Miner may represent in-app or virtual rewards. Availability, transferability, withdrawal eligibility, and processing are subject to the applicable Atomix Miner rules and verification requirements.
