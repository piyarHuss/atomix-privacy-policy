Privacy Policy - Atomix Miner

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Atomix Miner ("Atomix", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Atomix Miner application.

By using Atomix Miner, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Atomix Miner may collect or process the following information:

  • User ID or account identifier.
  • Profile information provided within the application.
  • Mining activity and mining session information.
  • Task, reward, and game activity.
  • Coin or token balances maintained by the application.
  • Transaction and withdrawal records.
  • Referral and invitation information.
  • Application settings and preferences.
  • Device or app-related information when required by advertising, security, or technical services.
  • Information required to prevent abuse, fraud, or manipulation of rewards.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • To operate and provide Atomix Miner features.
  • To maintain user accounts and application data.
  • To synchronize mining, task, reward, and wallet information.
  • To calculate and maintain rewards.
  • To process eligible transactions or withdrawal requests.
  • To maintain referral and invitation systems.
  • To detect cheating, abuse, fraud, or unauthorized activity.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the application.
  • To display and measure advertisements.
  • To troubleshoot technical problems and improve the application.

3. Firebase and Data Storage

Atomix Miner may use Firebase services, including Firebase Realtime Database, to store and synchronize application information.

Depending on the features enabled in the application, stored information may include user identifiers, balances, mining status, rewards, tasks, transactions, referrals, and other application-related data.

Firebase data is processed according to the configuration of the application and the applicable Firebase services.

4. Advertising

Atomix Miner may display advertisements through third-party advertising providers.

Advertising providers may collect certain device, advertising, technical, or interaction information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

The information collected by an advertising provider may be used for purposes such as:

  • Displaying advertisements.
  • Measuring advertising performance.
  • Preventing invalid or fraudulent advertising activity.
  • Providing relevant advertising where applicable.
Important: The final advertising provider listed in the Amazon Appstore privacy questionnaire must match the advertising SDKs actually included in the published APK.

5. Third-Party Services

Atomix Miner may use third-party services for functionality such as database storage, advertising, security, analytics, hosting, or other technical operations.

Third-party service providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We do not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services. Users should review the privacy policies of those services where appropriate.

6. Wallet, Tokens and Transactions

Atomix Miner may maintain in-app balances, token information, transaction records, and withdrawal information depending on the features enabled in the application.

Transaction-related information may be used to verify requests, maintain records, prevent abuse, and provide the related application functionality.

Minimum thresholds, eligibility requirements, processing times, and other transaction rules may be displayed inside the application and may change from time to time.

Atomix Miner does not guarantee the value, price, market value, or exchange rate of any in-app balance or token unless explicitly stated by us.

7. Rewards and Tasks

Information related to tasks, advertisements, reward claims, scores, mining activity, and other in-app actions may be recorded to calculate and maintain user rewards.

We may restrict, suspend, or remove rewards where we detect cheating, automation, manipulation, fraudulent activity, multiple-account abuse, or other violations of our rules.

8. Referral Program

If the application provides a referral or invitation system, referral identifiers and related activity may be processed to calculate eligible referral rewards.

Referral rewards are subject to the rules displayed in the application.

9. Fair Play and Account Security

Users are expected to use Atomix Miner fairly and only through supported methods.

The use of bots, automated scripts, emulators used to manipulate rewards, multiple accounts, exploits, or other methods intended to abuse the reward system may result in account restrictions, suspension, or forfeiture of pending rewards.

We do not sell users' personal information as a commercial product.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the application, including database, hosting, advertising, security, technical, or support providers.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, regulatory requirements, or to protect the security and rights of users, Atomix Miner, or others.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application, maintain transaction and reward records, prevent fraud and abuse, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and protect our legitimate interests.

13. Children's Privacy

Atomix Miner is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

14. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting our support team.

When a valid deletion request is received, we will review the request and delete or anonymize information where required by applicable law and where retention is not otherwise necessary.
